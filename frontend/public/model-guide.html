Generate a still from a prompt. Nail start/end frames here before you spend on video.

Flux 2 Pro

Best for photoreal stills

T2I

vision:flux 2 pro t2i

Strengths

  • Studio-grade photoreal. Default T2I in V2.
  • Reliable start/end frames before I2V.
  • Cheap versus a video miss.

Watch-outs

  • Not the cheapest Flux 2 SKU.
  • Type and infographics lose to Qwen / Recraft.

Use for

  • Listing hero stills
  • Start frame for I2V
  • Photoreal product plates

Best for cheap photoreal iteration

T2I

vision:flux 2 t2i

Strengths

  • Same Flux 2 family, faster/cheaper (~$0.012/MP).
  • Good explore-then-promote workflow.

Watch-outs

  • Softer than Pro on hard details.
  • Not the final delivery still.

Use for

  • Prompt fishing
  • Batch variants
  • Throwaway comps

Wan 3.0

T2V

  • Longer clips get expensive at 1080p.
  • No Prime SKU in this wiring.

  • Longer single takes
